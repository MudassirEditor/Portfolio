About Me

I Cut Footage Until It Stops People From Scrolling

Most raw footage is boring — even good footage. My job starts where the camera stops: finding the hook in the first second, trimming the dead air, syncing the cut to the beat until it feels less like a video and more like a reflex. Whether it's a 15-second UGC ad or a 20-minute YouTube long-form, the goal's the same — keep the thumb still.

I work across CapCut, DaVinci Resolve, Premiere Pro and Canva, and I lean on AI tools where they actually save time instead of just being a buzzword — captioning, rough cuts, ideation — never the final creative call. That's still mine. 🧠
